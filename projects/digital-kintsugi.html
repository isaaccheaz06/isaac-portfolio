Digital Fabrication

Digital Kintsugi

A project inspired by Japanese kintsugi. This project reinterprets kintsugi through a digital fabrication lens, exploring the intersection of traditional craft and modern technology. It combines ceramic 3D printing, intentional fracture, 3D scanning, Rhino modeling, and a custom 3D-printed PLA repair component to create a unique piece that celebrates the beauty of imperfection in a contemporary context.

Tools
Rhino · 3D Scanning · Ceramic 3D Printing · FDM 3D Printing
